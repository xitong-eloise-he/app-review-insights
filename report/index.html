Why do Duolingo users leave for other apps?

174k Google Play reviews, a 6.2k stratified sample tagged by an LLM for pain points, sentiment and switching intent, tracked by release and compared with Babbel, Busuu, Memrise and Speak. The findings feed a PRD.

Where switchers go

Competitor reviews written by people who say they came from Duolingo.

What they praise: grammar explanations, speaking / conversation practice, structured lessons, native-speaker audio, fewer ads and less gamification.

Competitors: what's wrong with the alternatives

250 random recent reviews per competitor, same tagging.

Babbel attracts the most switchers but has the angriest reviews of its own, mostly about price. Duolingo can win these users back with a better free learning experience. It doesn't have to match Babbel's prices.
